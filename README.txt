Know when your bins are emptied. Affald DK fetches the waste pickup calendar for your address from your Danish municipality's waste system and makes it available everywhere in Homey.

Add your address as a device and you get:
- Days until the next pickup of each waste type (residual waste, food waste, paper, glass/metal, ...), logged in Insights
- The next pickup with its date and waste types
- A flow trigger that reminds you at a chosen time, e.g. at 19:00 the evening before the paper bin is emptied
- Flow conditions such as "Paper is collected tomorrow" or "Any waste type is collected within 2 days"
- A dashboard widget showing the upcoming pickups with icons

Around 90 municipalities are supported through AffaldOnline, Perfect Waste, RenoWeb, Open Experience, Provas, RenoDjurs, RenoSyd, Vest Forbrænding, WasteWatch, Kredsløb (Aarhus), Odense Renovation, København, Herning, Ikast-Brande and Kolding.

Based on the Home Assistant integration Affaldshåndtering DK by Bjarne Riis and TermeHansen (github.com/briis/affalddk), used under the MIT license.
