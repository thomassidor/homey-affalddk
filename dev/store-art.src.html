Homey App Store · app images

Four directions for Affald DK's store art

The App Store shows these images at 1000×700, 500×350 and 250×175. The small one is in the app list, so each direction is judged there too. Everything uses the app's green and the waste type icons already in the app. All data is made up, and none of it uses the Homey logo, name or device.

A

Kalender

A month calendar where the pickup days are the waste type icons themselves, with the dashboard widget in front. It says what the app knows, and when, in one picture.

B

Widget

The product shot: the dashboard widget in dark and light, large and slightly turned. Closest to what people get, and it matches the widget previews.

C

Spande

An illustration: the street's row of bins in their fraction colours, one of them saying it's due tomorrow. The friendliest of the four, and it reads as waste collection even at 250 px.

D

Mosaik

A tilted wall of every waste type icon around the app's mark. Bold and colourful in the app list, with the least detail to get lost when it's small.
